## Read this before you post

**1. You asked for urgency and hype. You're getting urgency. You're not getting
hype, and here's why.**

Your own voice rules say hype reads as doubt on an offer this unusual, and the
invariants forbid manufactured scarcity. So I did not write "only 3 spots left",
a countdown, or a deadline — because none of those exist, and the first salon
owner who works that out stops believing the $400 too.

What I built instead is urgency from two things that are true:
- **Your reader's own calendar.** Count back three months from your busiest
  week. That's the window. Asserts nothing, and it lands.
- **The ongoing cost of not changing.** Four things that go wrong every week on
  the old setup. That's urgency about their situation, not my deadline.

Reel 2's caption says out loud that there's no countdown. On an account with no
followers yet, that line does more work than a fake one would.

**If there IS a real limit — a number of setups you can actually take on in
September — tell me the number and I'll use it.** Real scarcity is the most
persuasive thing available. Invented scarcity is the most expensive.

**2. Both Reels are silent, on purpose — and this corrects what I told you last
week.**

Last week I wrote into your working memory that your Creator account means you
can use trending chart audio. **That was wrong and I've withdrawn it.**
Instagram's label deals cover personal and creator use, not commercial use. Your
account type changes which tracks the picker *offers* you; it doesn't license a
business to use them. I could not verify this against Meta's own documentation —
their help centre renders client-side and returned nothing — so it's the
consistent reading of several sources rather than a confirmed fact, and it's
recorded that way.

**So: add audio from the commercially-cleared collection in the app, or post
them silent.** Both Reels carry the whole message on screen and work muted,
which is how most of the feed is watched anyway. A slightly less fashionable
track costs a little reach. A muted or struck post costs all of it.

**3. This week's one variable is format.** Last week was three carousels. This
week is two Reels, one single, one carousel — with voice, price, offer and CTA
all held constant, so any difference is format and not writing. That's the whole
point of the mix.
