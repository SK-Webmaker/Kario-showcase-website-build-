# Research — week of 2026-09-07

## The finding that corrected last week's advice

**`CLAUDE.md` told this account to use trending chart audio. That was wrong and
it has been withdrawn.**

Last week's note said: Creator account, so the trending library is available —
check it shows in the picker and film to it. The availability part is right. The
advice was not.

What multiple independent secondary sources agree on: Instagram's licensing
deals with labels cover **personal and creator** use, not commercial use.
Business accounts are limited to the smaller, commercially-cleared Meta Sound
Collection. Switching account type changes which tracks the picker *offers*; it
does not grant a commercial licence to a business promoting software, which is
what Kairo is.

**This is not verified against Meta's own documentation.** The Instagram help
centre renders client-side and returned no content when fetched, so this is the
consistent reading of secondary sources, not a primary-source fact, and it is
written down that way in `CLAUDE.md`.

**What the pack does about it:** both Reels are delivered **silent**, with the
whole message on screen. Most feed viewing is muted anyway. When adding audio in
the app, use the commercially-cleared collection. The downside is asymmetric — a
less fashionable track costs a little reach; a muted or struck post costs all of
it.

1. **The operator sends links.** They scroll Instagram; Gloss cannot. Two or
